# NEXCODE content and design guidance

## Design Context

### Users
Business owners and teams comparing a technology partner for custom software or an existing system. They need to understand fit, scope, and the next step without technical jargon. This audience is inferred from the company profile and current website.

### Brand Personality
Practical, attentive, clear. Explain what a service helps someone do; support trust with real work rather than superlatives or invented metrics.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the current dark visual identity, green accent, typography, and spacious layouts. This is a content and SEO improvement, not a visual rebrand.

### Design Principles
- Give every page one primary purpose and one clear primary heading.
- Let each section add information; remove repeated sales pitches and filler.
- Keep copy useful to nontechnical decision makers and actions explicit.
- Preserve readable layouts, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support.
- Ground company facts in the supplied LinkedIn profile; do not invent prices, credentials, clients, or delivery guarantees.
