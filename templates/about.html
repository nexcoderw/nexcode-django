A technology studio rooted in Kigali

NEXCODE was founded in 2019. From Norrsken House Kigali, we work with businesses and teammates across time zones to turn practical needs into digital products.

Close to the people who use the work

Our work spans websites, mobile applications and smart-card platforms. We have worked across finance, hospitality, construction, travel and public safety, where clear information and dependable workflows matter every day.

We listen to the people doing the work, make the idea tangible through design, and build around the task it needs to support. The team behind the product remains a point of contact as it evolves.
