Before we start

What should I bring to the first conversation?

Describe the problem, who the product is for, and any deadline or budget constraints. Screens, examples and a short description of your current process are helpful; a technical specification is not required.

Do I need a custom build?

Not always. If an existing system fits your needs, ask about a managed setup. A custom build is useful when your workflows or integrations need a more specific approach.

How are cost and timing agreed?

We discuss features, dependencies and delivery priorities before proposing a scope. Changes to that scope are reviewed together so their effect on cost and timing is clear.

What happens after launch?

Hosting, updates and support are agreed as part of the project or a separate maintenance plan. Tell us who will manage the product day to day so the handover fits your team.
